# Login pages

Rules for login, sign-in, sign-up, authentication, password reset, and account access pages.

- Never use light gray or other light-tinted backgrounds (e.g. `bg-gray-50`, `bg-gray-100`, `bg-slate-50`) on login/sign-in pages. Use solid white (`bg-white`) or dark (`bg-gray-900`, `bg-gray-950`, `bg-black`), unless the form content sits inside its own panel or card
