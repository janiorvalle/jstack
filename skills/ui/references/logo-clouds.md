# Logo clouds

Rules for logo grids, customer logos, partner logos, trust bars, client rows, and any collection of brand marks.

- Always spread logos evenly across rows when they wrap. Never leave an unbalanced last row (e.g. 5 on one row and 1 on the next). Use a grid or layout that splits logos as evenly as possible across all rows (e.g. 3+3 instead of 5+1 for 6 logos)
- A logo cloud right under a hero is part of the hero. Match the hero's alignment. A left-aligned hero gets a left-aligned logo cloud label and logos
